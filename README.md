Controls:

←/→ Move Flipper Left and Right.

Mantain ↓ to kick the ball with the kicker.

Q/E Spin the Ball, Left and Right.

Press 1 to decrease the gravity.

Press 2 to increase the gravity.

Press 3 to decrease the fps.

Press 4 to increase the fps.

Press 5 to decrease the bumper power.

Press 6 to increase the bumper power.
